# Mandi — Project Overview & Executive Problem Definition

## 1. Project Vision
Mandi is an enterprise-grade multi-tenant B2B AgTech ERP and digital trading platform designed specifically for the agricultural commission grain markets (Ghalla Mandi / غلہ منڈی) across Pakistan and South Asia.

## 2. Core Problem Statement
Traditional Mandi operations suffer from:
1. **Manual Paper Bahi-Khata (بہی کھاتہ):** Vulnerable to clerical math errors, dispute disputes between Arhtis and Zamindars, and physical register damage.
2. **Opacity in Live Auctions (نیلامی بولی):** Bids shouted verbally often lead to disputes over winning price and allocated weight.
3. **Weight Discrepancies:** Manual weighbridge calculations between gross weight and tare weight in Maunds (من) and KG cause financial leakage.
4. **Delayed Settlements:** Farmers wait days or weeks for written statements detailing their crop proceeds, deductions, and advances.
5. **Lack of Tenant Security:** Multi-shop businesses operating across different cities lack unified multi-tenancy and audit logs.

## 3. Targeted Stakeholders
1. **Commission Shop Owners (آڑھتی / مالک):** Executive KPI oversight, real-time Roznamcha, P&L, partner drawings, and permission security.
2. **Shop Clerks & Munshis (ہیڈ منشی و کیشیئر):** Rapid Boli entry, invoice generation, thermal cash receipt printing, and cheque clearing.
3. **Farmers / Zamindars (زمیندار):** Digital passbooks, real-time crop proceeds tracking, and advance loan recovery transparency.
4. **Buyers / Vyaparis (خریدار / بیوپاری):** Itemized bills, gate-out transport tokens, and volume loyalty rebates.
5. **Weighbridge & Security Staff (کانٹا و گیٹ انچارج):** Digital Kanda gross/tare computation and inward/outward gate pass enforcement.
