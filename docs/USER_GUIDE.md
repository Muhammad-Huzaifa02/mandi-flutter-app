# Mandi App — End-User Operational Manual (رہنمائی)

This operational guide explains how **Shop Owners**, **Accountants (Munshi / منشی)**, **Sales Staff**, **Buyers (Customers)**, and **Farmers (Suppliers)** interact with the Mandi Application.

---

## 1. Shop Registration & Setup Wizard

### For New Shop Owners:
1. Open the app and tap **Create a New Mandi Shop**.
2. Enter your Full Name, Mobile Number, Email, and Password.
3. Complete the **Shop Setup Wizard**:
   - Enter Shop Name, City (e.g. Multan, Faisalabad, Sargodha), and Address.
   - Upload Shop Logo image.
   - Set Default Commission % (e.g. `5.0%`) and Invoice Prefix (e.g. `INV`).

---

## 2. Creating Sales Invoices & Maund Calculations

### Step-by-Step Invoice Flow:
1. Tap **Invoices** tile on the Dashboard → Tap **+ Create Invoice**.
2. Select Customer (Buyer) or leave as Walk-in.
3. Select Product (e.g. Wheat / گندم, Rice / چاول).
4. Enter **Number of Bags** and **Total Weight in KG**.
   - The app automatically calculates weight in **Manns (من = 40 KG)**: e.g. 100 KG = 2.50 Manns.
5. Enter **Unit Price per Mann**.
6. Enter Line Expenses (Labor/Mazdoori, Freight/Kraya).
7. Review total, enter received cash amount, and tap **Create Invoice**.

---

## 3. Thermal Printing & Receipts

1. Open Invoice Detail Page.
2. Tap the **Receipt Icon** in the top bar to print an **80mm or 58mm Thermal POS Slip**.
3. Tap **WhatsApp** button to send instant PDF receipt directly to the buyer's WhatsApp.

---

## 4. Voiding / Cancelling an Invoice

If an incorrect entry was created or goods were returned:
1. Open Invoice Detail Page.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Void / Cancel Invoice**.
3. Enter the cancellation reason and confirm.
4. Product stock is automatically returned to inventory, and customer running balance is restored.

---

## 5. Khata & Customer Balances

1. Tap **Customers** tile on the Dashboard.
2. Search for customer name or phone number.
3. Tap on customer to view complete **Running Balance Ledger**.
4. Tap **Export Statement** to generate A4 PDF account statement for printing or WhatsApp sharing.
5. Tap **Record Payment** when cash/cheque is received from buyer.

---

## 6. Financial Reports & Exports

1. Tap **Reports** tile on the Dashboard.
2. Choose period filter: **Today, This Week, This Month, or Custom Date Range**.
3. Tap the **Export Icon** in the top bar to download complete financial summary to **Excel (.xlsx)** or **PDF**.
